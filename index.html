How to play

  1. Choose a picture and difficulty.
  2. Study the finished picture, then press Start. The timer begins.
  3. Drag a piece with your mouse or finger onto the grid.
  4. When a piece is close to its spot, it snaps in. The timer stops with the last piece.
Tip

Start with the corner and edge pieces: they have a flat side.
